Our Story

Built by people who understand fire service.

OpenFirehouse was born out of frustration with the status quo — expensive, complicated software that required a dedicated IT administrator and a full-time office staff just to keep running.

We built something different. A platform that's powerful enough to run a large career department, but simple enough that a volunteer Chief can set it up on a Sunday afternoon and have the whole crew using it by Monday.

Every feature, every screen, every workflow was designed with real fire departments in mind — from the first-due engine company to the battalion command post.
