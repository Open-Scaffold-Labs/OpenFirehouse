Usage Rules

RuleDetails
The cube is sacredNever modify the cube faces, scaffold lines, or amber threads. They are identical across all products.
Only the wrapper changesProduct identity lives in the wrapper — color and shape. The cube never changes.
Document logoUse the -white.png variant for Word/PDF documents on light backgrounds.
App logoUse the transparent .png variant for dark UI backgrounds.
Minimum sizeNever render below 32×32px. Recommended: 64px for documents, 140px for presentations.
Document title blockLogo goes in the left cell of the Electric Indigo title block. 64px display size.
Font pairingTimes New Roman for documents. Inter for UI/web. Never Arial.
